# CECRL Domain Rules

Apply these rules when writing test data, writing validator schemas, or working with CECRL evaluation grids.

## CECRL Terminology & Concepts

- **Levels (A1 to C2)**: Understand and use the 6 Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CECRL) levels: `A1`, `A2`, `B1`, `B2`, `C1`, `C2`.
- **Language Skills**: Focus on the 5 standard skills:
  - CE: Compréhension Écrite (Reading comprehension)
  - CO: Compréhension Orale (Listening comprehension)
  - EE: Écriture / Expression Écrite (Written expression)
  - EOC: Expression Orale en Continu (Continuous speaking)
  - EOI: Expression Orale en Interaction (Interactive speaking)
- **Sessions & Cycle**: The evaluation cycle spans 6 sessions (`S1` to `S6`) over a 3-year French vocational high school program (lycée professionnel).
- **French UI Language**: All client/user-facing text must be in French. Use proper technical terms for language teaching (e.g. *Compréhension écrite* instead of *Reading*).
- **Realistic Progression**: When generating test data or assessing progression, assume realistic learning curves where a student progresses by 1 to 2 CEFR levels over the course of 3 years.
